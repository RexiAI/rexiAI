# AC-020: FAQ — four honest, generic questions

## AC-020-19 — Exactly four honest Q/A pairs in both locales
Given the FAQ section renders in either locale
Then exactly four question/answer pairs render
And the four topics are covered: maintenance cost, what if it breaks, who owns
  the data, does it work on mobile
And no answer contains an invented SLA (no `%` uptime, no `99.9`), an invented
  price (no amount other than the public `$1,200` / `$600`), or an invented
  metric
